Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about buying an SMDC Tagaytay property

SMDC Tagaytay offers flexible payment schemes including: (1) Spot Cash — full payment with a discount; (2) In-house Installment — pay directly to SMDC over 5–10 years at 0% or low interest; (3) Bank Financing — through partner banks such as BDO, BPI, Metrobank, and PNB with up to 20-year loan terms; and (4) Pag-IBIG Financing — available for qualified Filipino buyers. A typical reservation fee starts at ₱10,000–₱20,000.

The reservation process is straightforward: (1) Choose your preferred unit and development; (2) Submit required documents (2 valid IDs, TIN, proof of income); (3) Pay the reservation fee (₱10,000–₱20,000); (4) Sign the Reservation Agreement; (5) Your unit is officially held while you prepare documents for the chosen payment scheme. Our property consultants will assist you at every step.

Turnover schedules vary by development: Cool Suites and Hope Residences are Ready for Occupancy (RFO) and can be turned over shortly after full payment or loan approval. Leaf Residences and Shore 3 Residences are under pre-selling and are expected to be turned over in 2026–2028 depending on the phase. All turnover dates are stated in your Contract to Sell.

Yes! Foreign nationals are allowed to own condominium units in the Philippines under the Philippine Condominium Act (R.A. 4726), as long as foreign ownership in the building does not exceed 40%. SMDC Tagaytay welcomes both local and international buyers. Payment must be remitted via inward remittance through BSP-accredited banks. Our team can assist OFW and foreign buyers with the documentation process.

After turnover, unit owners pay: (1) Monthly Dues (Association Dues) — typically ₱80–₱120 per sqm per month, covering common area maintenance, security, and amenities upkeep; (2) Real Property Tax — annual tax payable to the local government; (3) Utilities — metered electricity and water billed separately. For investment units, rental income typically covers the monthly dues and mortgage payments.

Short-term rental policies vary by development and are governed by the House Rules and Condominium Corporation. Generally, SMDC Tagaytay units in tourist-heavy developments like Cool Suites and Shore 3 Residences have provisions for short-term leasing, making them ideal for Airbnb-style rental income. Please inquire with our team about the specific policies for your preferred development.
