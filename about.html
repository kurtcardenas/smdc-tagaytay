About SMDC Tagaytay

Delivering world-class highland living experiences in Tagaytay City, Philippines

Our Story

SM Development Corporation

SM Development Corporation (SMDC) is the real estate arm of SM Prime Holdings, Inc. — one of the largest integrated property developers in Southeast Asia. Since its founding, SMDC has been committed to developing vibrant, inclusive, and sustainable communities across the Philippines.

With a portfolio spanning Metro Manila, key provincial cities, and now the cool highlands of Tagaytay, SMDC brings its hallmark of quality construction, innovative design, and exceptional customer service to every development. Our Tagaytay properties are designed to harmonize with the natural landscape while providing residents with all the conveniences of modern urban living.

Location Advantage

Why Choose Tagaytay?

Discover why Tagaytay is the Philippines' most sought-after highland residential destination

Cool Year-Round Climate

Tagaytay sits at 700 meters above sea level, giving it a naturally cool temperature of 18–24°C all year — perfect for relaxation and a healthy lifestyle.

Close Proximity to Manila

Only ~60 km from Makati CBD via SLEX and Tagaytay–Nasugbu Highway. Perfect for weekend getaways or as a primary residence for those working in the South.

Premier Tourist Destination

Known for its iconic Taal Lake views, world-famous restaurants, and vibrant tourism industry — ideal for generating rental income from short-term stays.

Rising Property Values

Tagaytay real estate has consistently appreciated by 8–12% annually, making it one of the Philippines' top performing real estate investment markets.
