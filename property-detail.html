About This Property

Cool Suites Tagaytay is SMDC's premier condominium development nestled in the cool highlands of Tagaytay City. This thoughtfully designed residential community offers studio and one-bedroom units with modern interiors, floor-to-ceiling windows, and breathtaking views of the Taal Lake and volcano.

Surrounded by lush greenery and cool mountain breeze averaging 18–24°C year-round, Cool Suites provides the ideal escape from Metro Manila's hustle and bustle — just 2 hours away via Tagaytay–Nasugbu Highway. Whether as a primary residence, vacation home, or investment property, Cool Suites delivers exceptional value and lifestyle.
